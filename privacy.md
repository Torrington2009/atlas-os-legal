# Atlas OS Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 11 November 2026**

## 1. About Atlas OS

Atlas OS is a privately developed Windows desktop dashboard intended exclusively for personal, non-commercial use by its developer.

It brings together personal information and productivity tools, including financial tracking, savings, subscriptions, assignments and other dashboard features.

Atlas OS is not currently offered as a public service.

## 2. Information processed

Depending on the features being used, Atlas OS may process:

- Bank account names and account identifiers.
- Account balances and available balances.
- Transaction descriptions, dates and amounts.
- Savings balances and interest information.
- Financial information manually entered by the user.
- Application settings, widget layouts and preferences.

## 3. Open Banking

Atlas OS is being developed to support read-only Open Banking connections through Enable Banking.

If implemented and authorised, this functionality may retrieve account information and transaction history from supported financial institutions.

The intended functionality does not include initiating payments, transferring money or making changes to bank accounts.

Bank authentication is performed through the relevant financial institution's authorisation process. Atlas OS is not designed to request or collect banking passwords.

## 4. Data storage

Atlas OS is designed primarily as a locally operated Windows application.

Application settings and manually entered information may be stored on the user's device.

Any future banking integration will require appropriate handling and protection of authentication credentials, access tokens and retrieved financial information.

## 5. Third-party services

Atlas OS may communicate with third-party providers where required to deliver specific functionality.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

Atlas OS does not sell personal financial information or use it for advertising.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by Atlas OS may remain on the user's device until it is deleted.

Users should be able to remove locally stored application information and revoke banking authorisation through the relevant provider or financial institution.

Third-party providers may retain information in accordance with their own policies.

## 7. Security

Reasonable technical measures will be used to protect information processed by Atlas OS.

The application is under active development, and security measures may change as features are introduced.

Users should maintain appropriate security on their Windows devices and protect any application credentials or private keys.

This policy may be updated as Atlas OS develops. The latest version will be available at this address.
