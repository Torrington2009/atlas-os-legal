# Atlas OS Terms of Use

**Last updated: 11 November 2026**

## 1. Purpose

Atlas OS is a privately developed Windows desktop dashboard intended exclusively for personal, non-commercial use.

It is designed to help organise personal information, finances, savings, subscriptions, assignments and productivity tools.

## 2. Personal use

Atlas OS is currently intended for use by its developer only.

It is not offered as a commercial product, public banking service or financial advisory service.

## 3. Open Banking functionality

Atlas OS is being developed to support read-only Open Banking connections through Enable Banking.

Where available and authorised, the application may retrieve account balances, transaction history and other permitted account information.

Atlas OS is not designed to:
- Initiate payments.
- Transfer money.
- Set up direct debits.
- Modify bank account details.
- Perform banking transactions.

Access depends on the availability of participating financial institutions and the relevant provider's requirements.

## 4. Accuracy of financial information

Information displayed by Atlas OS may originate from banks, third-party providers or manual user entries.

Information may be delayed, incomplete or temporarily unavailable.

Atlas OS should not be treated as the definitive record of a financial account. Important financial information should be verified directly with the relevant financial institution.

## 5. Third-party services

Certain features may rely on third-party services, including Enable Banking.

Those services are governed by their own terms, policies and availability.

Atlas OS cannot guarantee uninterrupted access to third-party services.

## 6. Security

Atlas OS is under active development, and its security arrangements may evolve as functionality is introduced.

## 7. Development and availability

No guarantee is made that the application will operate without interruption or errors.

## 8. Financial decisions

It does not provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
